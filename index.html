Common questions about your website

Short answers, no small print.

How much does a small business website cost in South Africa?

Starter (one page) is R2 500, Standard (up to five pages) is R4 500 and Plus (up to 10 pages with one interactive feature) is R8 500, once-off, with no VAT added. Bigger or interactive websites are Custom, from R15 000 by written quote. Extras: Google Business Profile pack R950, care plan R350 a month or R3 500 a year. These are summer-special prices for orders up to and including 28 February 2027; the normal prices are R3 500, R5 600 and R10 000.

How long does it take?

Starter and Standard: within 48 hours of receiving your deposit and final details (photos, logo, opening hours, services). Plus: 5 to 7 working days. Custom: usually 1 to 3 weeks, with the timeline in writing in the quote.

What do I pay, and when?

The demo is free. If you say yes, you pay a 50% deposit and we build your website. You pay the balance when you approve the finished website, before it goes live on your own domain.

What if I don’t like the demo?

Then you simply say no and pay nothing. The demo is free.

Can you build my website in Afrikaans?

Yes. Every Get Online SA website comes in Afrikaans and English, so your customers can read it in the language they prefer.

Will my website show up on Google?

We build every website so Google can read it: mobile-friendly, with clear page titles and descriptions. Standard includes a Google Business Profile clean-up and basic local search set-up. Nobody can promise a Google ranking and we do not, but a well-built website with a good Google Business Profile is how small businesses get found. Google verification times are set by Google and can take longer than 48 hours.

Do I need to be technical to get a website?

No. You send us photos, your logo, opening hours and services (or we use what your business has already made public), and we do the rest, including setting up the domain and hosting.

Do I own my website?

Yes, once you have paid in full. Your domain is registered in your own name.

What does it cost after the first payment?

Only the domain (roughly R50 to R150 a year, paid to the registrar). Hosting is included for 12 months. After that the care plan covers it, or you take the files.

I want a bigger website, like Mountain View Village.

Tell us what you need. Plus (R8 500) covers up to 10 pages with one interactive feature. Bigger than that is Custom, from R15 000 by quote, with a written timeline before you pay. You can also start with Standard, go live in 48 hours, and expand later in phases.

Can I make changes later?

One round is included. After that you can take the care plan (30 minutes of changes a month) or we quote first.
